## Credits
Doppar is a modern PHP framework created by [Mahedi Hasan](https://github.com/techmahedy), built with a focus on simplicity, speed, and real-world scalability. Designed to handle everything from small applications to systems dealing with billions of records, Doppar is engineered for developers who value performance and clean architecture.

Many of Doppar’s foundational elements, such as class libraries, helpers, and sub-systems are using the robust and battle-tested architecture of [Symfony](https://symfony.com), while introducing unique innovations tailored to streamline development and reduce complexity.

Many of the function names in Doppar are heavily inspired by [Laravel](https://laravel.com), one of the most popular PHP frameworks. We deeply admire the elegance and clarity Laravel brings to development, and its influence is clearly reflected in our work.

At the same time, Doppar would be unimaginable without the solid foundation provided by Symfony. We are immensely grateful to the Laravel community for their contributions to the developer ecosystem, and we're proud to incorporate some of their function naming conventions in Doppar, as a small tribute to their impact.

Doppar continues to evolve with community feedback, modern best practices, and a commitment to developer happiness.
